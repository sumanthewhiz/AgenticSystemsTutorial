## How this tutorial works

Every chapter teaches one set of production concepts in four moves:

:::flow One chapter
Understand | the concept, from first principles
Locate | where SwarmPipe implements it
Do | a hands-on lab with real commands
Break | switch the defense off and watch what fails
:::

You'll need about 20 minutes of setup ([Chapter 0](chapters/00-welcome.html)). After that, everything runs locally
and offline, with no API keys and no cloud account. SwarmPipe's simulated models make every failure reproducible,
and you can switch to a real local model whenever you like.

:::tip Your progress is saved
Mark chapters complete as you go. Progress is kept in this browser and shown in the top bar and on the chapter cards
below. Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere to search, and use <kbd>&larr;</kbd> / <kbd>&rarr;</kbd> to move between chapters.
:::
